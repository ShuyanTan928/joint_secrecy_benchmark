A role is any of these: their job, position, department, desk, team, or profession; their place in a reporting line (whose manager they are, whom they report to); a role in society (a board member, a volunteer coordinator, a church elder, a club officer, a student); or a relationship (someone's wife, husband, parent, child, sibling, partner, ex). "Fix" means that a reader would know it from this email. A title in a signature fixes it. "I have approved the invoices for my team" fixes it. "My wife and I" fixes it. A greeting, a thank-you, a book recommendation, a forwarded article, a question anyone at the company could ask, fixes nothing. Talk of work or of other people in general does not fix it unless it says what the writer's own place is.
