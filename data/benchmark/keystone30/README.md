# keystone30

Thirty planted secrets in the Keystone mailbox, the first set generated end to end by the pipeline in this
repository, on 2026-09-29.

- `state.json`: the generation state, what the tester reads: the 10 secrets, the 30 chains with their parts, plans,
  emails, cast and the AND check's rounds (every probe, candidate and verdict).
- `mailbox.jsonl`: the released background (`data/release/background_2000.jsonl`, 2,000 emails, 1,007 threads) with
  the 30 chains' threads placed by date; 2,181 emails in 1,092 threads.
- `answer_key.json`: per chain, the secret, the actor and the victim as cast, the pattern, the kind and the planted
  thread ids.

Topics: law, careers (work), personal finance, family and relationships (life); kinds: dark, entrusted and strategic
(strategic only where the graded pool holds it: careers, family and relationships); patterns: lying by commission,
paltering, lying by omission. Generated by Claude Opus 5.5 (GPT-6 Sol on content-filtered calls), checked by GPT-6 Sol
on every subset of each chain and by Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 3.7 Flash on the full set, judged by GPT-6 Sol, with
one fix round. 25 chains sit on three threads, 5 on two.

Run the tester on it:

    python scripts/run_testers.py --models openrouter/anthropic/claude-opus-5.5,openrouter/openai/gpt-6-sol \
        --judge-model openrouter/openai/gpt-6-sol --state data/benchmark/keystone30/state.json --n-controls 30

Results go to `results/tester/<model>/` and the table to `results/tester/table.md`.
